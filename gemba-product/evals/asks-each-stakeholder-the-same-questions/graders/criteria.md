The meeting has three attendees: the owner and his partner, who hold different visions, and the mechanic.

PASS if the kit asks the owner and the partner the same questions about the point where their visions differ, and writes them separately for each one (a block, heading or labeled list per person), so each answers on their own and the other listens or answers apart.
FAIL if the kit asks those questions once to the group, if it asks the owner and the partner different questions about the disagreement, or if it has no questions for one of the two.
