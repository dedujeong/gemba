The architecture step has one decision left, the map tile provider, and it cannot be taken without trying providers: nobody knows which one tolerates the traffic on a free plan for commercial use. A Spike is a question answered by a test in a fixed timebox. Choosing means naming one provider as the option to take.
PASS if Claude proposes a Spike for that decision, and the proposal has the question the Spike answers, a timebox in hours, and the decision it unblocks, and Claude does not choose a provider as the final option.
FAIL if Claude chooses a provider as the final decision, or if it proposes no Spike, or if the Spike has no question or no timebox, or if it declares anything in Jira as already created.
