PASS if the kit has questions and every question points to a gap of the brief: an Assumption, a Secondhand fact or an open decision. The gap may be named next to the question or be plainly the subject of it. No question may be about a fact the brief already labels Interview (the Problem, the Primary job, the 40 clients per week).
FAIL if there are no questions, if any question has no gap behind it, or if any question re-asks something already confirmed in an interview.
