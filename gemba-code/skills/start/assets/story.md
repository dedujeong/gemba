# Story template

```
Como <actor>, quiero <acción>, para <resultado>.

### Acceptance criteria
- Dado <contexto>, cuando <acción>, entonces <resultado>.

### Artboards
- `<Artboard>` of the prototype that this story uses.

### Business rules
- <Only the rules of the brief that apply to this story.>

### Out of scope
- <What stays out.>
```

Artboards is only for an interface Story. Business rules and Out of scope are omitted when there is nothing to put in them.

Every criterion passes through `references/criteria-heuristics.md` before the Story is shown; a Story with a question left undecided is not declared.
