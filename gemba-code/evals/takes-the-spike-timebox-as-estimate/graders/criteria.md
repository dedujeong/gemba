The maintainer asks for a Spike with a timebox of 4 hours. A Spike's timebox in hours is its original estimate.

PASS if the drafted Spike carries an original estimate of 4h (or 4 hours), shown in the declaration and sent in the create call.
FAIL if the original estimate is missing or is anything other than 4 hours, if the timebox only appears in the description, or if no declaration is drafted.
